The growth ecosystem

One community.
Five dimensions of progress.

The developing platform brings several mechanisms together under one sponsor network, while tracking different forms of participation, qualification and progression.

01 / MATRIX

General Harvest

A shared matrix where qualifying members contribute to the progression of a larger community structure.

02 / TEAM

Team Harvest

An individual team matrix that develops as qualifying members join your organization over time.

03 / LEADERSHIP

Royalty

A five-stage leadership ladder from Member to Director, based on defined team-development milestones.

04 / PRODUCT ACTIVITY

Unilevel

A repeat-purchase mechanism connecting product activity with a multi-level reward structure and monthly activity criteria.

05 / REFERRAL

Indirect Referral

A structured mechanism that recognizes the wider sponsor network through multiple upline levels.

One sponsor network, several distinct mechanisms.The simulator models General Harvest, Team Harvest, Royalty, Unilevel and Indirect Referral separately. Their qualification triggers and calculations are not interchangeable. Explore the sandbox for the prototype logic; the production program's final terms and any compensation rules must be published separately.

A few things to know

Clarity comes first.

Eco-wealth is developing its company presentation and digital participation platform. These answers explain the current preview without presenting the sandbox as a finished commercial program.

Is this the finished Eco-wealth platform?

No. This website introduces the company vision and gives a preliminary look at the growth concept. The production platform is still being developed.

Are the simulator figures actual payouts?

No. The simulator is an experimental, browser-based sandbox. Its figures and calculations are illustrative only; it does not process real purchases, financial transactions or payouts.

What products does Eco-wealth offer?

This landing page does not publish a product catalogue. Specific product names, descriptions, ingredients, prices and availability should be checked against the official product information when it is released.

How does the growth model work?

The current simulator demonstrates five separate mechanisms: General Harvest, Team Harvest, Royalty, Unilevel and Indirect Referral. They share a sponsor network but have different qualification and activity rules. The simulator is the best place to explore how the prototype behaves.

Does registration or inviting someone guarantee compensation?

No compensation or income is guaranteed. The simulator includes an Indirect Referral calculation triggered by registration, alongside separate matrix, leadership and repeat-purchase calculations. Any real-world eligibility, amounts and payment conditions must be stated in the finalized official program rules.

Is this an investment offer?

This page presents a wellness, product-participation and livelihood concept. It does not offer an investment, promise returns or guarantee earnings. Review official program terms before making any participation or purchase decision.

Does the company make medical claims about its products?

This preview makes no claim that any product diagnoses, treats, cures or prevents disease. Product-specific claims should be supported by the official label and applicable product authorizations.
