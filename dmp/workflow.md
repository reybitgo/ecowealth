## **Phase 1: Registration & Account Setup**

### **Step 1.1: New Member Registration**

**Scenario:** Maria wants to join ECOWEALTH as a new associate. Her sponsor is Juan Cruz (username: **juan_cruz**).

**Important:** Sponsor must be identified by **username only** (not full name) because multiple users can have similar full names.

**How Registration Codes Work:**
- Registration codes are pre-purchased for cash by sponsors (existing associates)
- Each code is tied to a specific package (Package A or Package B)
- **Package A Registration Code:** ₱4,498.00 (paid by sponsor in advance)
- **Package B Registration Code:** ₱6,998.00 (paid by sponsor in advance)
- When used, the code automatically activates the user as a paid member with that package

**Registration Code Format:**
- **8-character alphanumeric code** (case-sensitive)
- **Non-ambiguous character set** for easy recognition when handwritten or printed:
  - **Full character set:** `ABDEFGHJMNPQRTVXYZabdefghjmnpqrtvxyz23456789`
  - **Total characters:** 47 (excludes: C, I, K, L, O, S, U, W, c, i, k, l, o, s, u, w, 0, 1)
  - **Why these exclusions:** Prevents confusion (I/1/l, O/0, S/5, etc.)
- **Example codes:** 
  - Package A: **A3dN7mR5** (₱4,498.00)
  - Package B: **B9hM6tQ2** (₱6,998.00)
- **First character** indicates package type: 
  - **A** (uppercase) = Package A
  - **B** (uppercase) = Package B
- **Remaining 7 characters** are randomly generated from the 47-character set
- **Why case-sensitive:** Increases security and uniqueness (47 possible characters per position)

**Registration Process:**
1. Juan Cruz (username: **juan_cruz**) purchases a **Package A Registration Code** for ₱4,498.00 cash
2. System generates code: **A3dN7mR5** (all characters from allowed set)
3. Juan gives this code to Maria (can be written clearly or sent digitally)
4. Maria visits the registration portal and enters:
   - **Username:** maria_cruz (must be unique)
   - **Password:** (her chosen password)
   - **Sponsor Username:** juan_cruz (REQUIRED - must be exact username, not full name)
   - **Registration Code:** A3dN7mR5 (must match case exactly)
5. System validates:
   - Registration code is valid and unused
   - Sponsor username exists (juan_cruz)
   - Username (maria_cruz) is unique
6. Maria is automatically registered as a **paid associate** with Package A
7. Maria receives:
   - Associate ID: **20251106-001**
   - Package A products (1 Coffee, 1 Immune Booster, 2 Spirulina, 1 Serpentina, 2 Kojic Soap)
   - 1-Year Free Insurance
   - Access to associate portal
   - Ability to purchase her own registration codes for recruiting

**System Actions:**
- Registration code is marked as "used" and linked to Maria's account (username: maria_cruz)
- Maria is placed in Juan's (username: juan_cruz) Level 1 downline
- Juan's account is credited with Direct Referral Bonus
- Maria can now purchase registration codes to recruit her own team
- Genealogy tree is built using usernames, not full names

### **Strategic Importance**

**Why QA Status is Critical:**
- **99% of income** depends on maintaining QA status
- Lost income is **permanent** - cannot be recovered under any circumstances
- **Every hour without QA** means potential income loss from downline activity
- Only Harvest milestone claims are deferred (not lost), all other bonuses are lost in real-time
- Restoration is **immediate** once ₱3,000 retail quota is met, but past losses remain

**The Cost of One Non-QA Day:**
If your downline is active, losing QA for even ONE day can cost you:
- **Indirect Referral (Package-Based Unilevel):** From any new packages purchased that day
- **Residual Income (Product-Based Unilevel):** From any product reorders that day  
- **Partnership Bonus:** If your upline has a big earning event that day
- **Royalty Bonus:** A portion of your monthly royalty

**Two Types of Unilevel:**
1. **Package-Based (Phase 4):** New associate registrations - explicit QA check
2. **Product-Based (Phase 6):** Product repurchases - QA inherently maintained through retail requirement

**Best Practice:**
- Set up **automatic monthly orders** with retail customers (prevent any gap)
- Maintain **buffer** (aim for ₱4,000-₱5,000, not just ₱3,000 minimum)
- Track retail customer orders **weekly**, not monthly
- Have **4-5 retail customers** (not just 3) as backup
- Set calendar reminders 5 days before month-end to verify quota
- **Never let QA lapse** - the cost is too high when downline is active

## **Phase 5: Harvest Program Milestones (Chronological Stacking System)**

**What Makes Harvest Different:** Unlike the Indirect Referral Bonus (which follows unilevel genealogy), Harvest uses a **chronological queue system** that ignores who recruited whom. Everyone in your entire downline organization is stacked in the order they registered.

**Package Equality:** Package A and Package B are counted equally - 1 package = 1 package, regardless of price.

### **Step 5.1: Understanding Chronological Stacking**

**How the Queue Works:**
1. Every person who joins your entire organization (regardless of who recruited them) gets a sequential position number
2. Positions fill chronologically based on registration timestamp
3. Matrix levels are filled in order:
   - **Harvest Level 1:** Positions 1-3 (first 3 people)
   - **Harvest Level 2:** Positions 4-12 (next 9 people)
   - **Harvest Level 3:** Positions 13-39 (next 27 people)
   - And so on...

**Example of Chronological Stacking:**

Let's say Juan's organization grows in this order:

| Registration Order | Person (Username) | Recruited By (Username) | Unilevel Level from Juan | Harvest Position | Harvest Matrix Level |

| 2nd                | Anna (anna_reyes) | Juan (juan_cruz)        | Level 1 (Direct)         | Position 2       | Matrix Level 1       |
| 3rd                | Peter (peter_tan) | Maria (maria_cruz)      | Level 2                  | Position 3       | Matrix Level 1       |
| 4th                | Carlos (carlos_m) | Juan (juan_cruz)        | Level 1 (Direct)         | Position 4       | Matrix Level 2       |
| 5th                | Sarah (sarah_lee) | Anna (anna_reyes)       | Level 2                  | Position 5       | Matrix Level 2       |
| 6th                | David (david_ng)  | Maria (maria_cruz)      | Level 2                  | Position 6       | Matrix Level 2       |
