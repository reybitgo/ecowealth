### **Whitepaper: The ECOWEALTH "Balanced Growth" Compensation Plan (V3.0)**

**Subject:** A Strategic Redesign for Sustainable, Long-Term Prosperity  
**Document Type:** Prescriptive Compensation Plan Model

### 1.0 Executive Summary

This document outlines the **"Balanced Growth" (V3.0)** compensation plan, a strategic evolution of the ECOWEALTH model. The original plan excels at "sizzle"—creating excitement and motivating rapid recruitment through tangible incentives and large bonus projections. However, this front-loaded energy comes at the cost of long-term sustainability, creating risks of market saturation, "inventory loading", and member passivity.

The V3.0 plan solves this by introducing a **"product-first"** foundation without "decapitating" the appeal. It masterfully links the plan's most attractive rewards to sustainable, healthy business activities. This model is designed for long-term legal compliance, member retention, and stable corporate growth.

### 3.0 Pillar 2: The "Fast Start" (The Sizzle)

To create immediate excitement and motivation, the "fast start" rewards are simple, tangible, and achievable.

- **Direct "Ayuda" Bonus:** A **₱500** bonus is paid for every new personally-sponsored associate.
- **First Trio Bonus:** Upon recruiting their first three (3) associates, the sponsor receives:

  - A one-time bonus of **5-Kilos of Rice**.

### 4.0 Pillar 3: The "Retail Gate" (The Sustainable Foundation)

This is the most critical component of the V3.0 plan, ensuring a "product-first" culture. To unlock the plan's deeper team bonuses, a member must become a "Qualified Associate" (QA).

- **How to Become a QA:** An associate must acquire and maintain **three (3) personal retail customers**.
- **Minimum Purchase Requirement:** Each retail customer must make a minimum monthly purchase of **₱1,000** worth of products (or ₱3,000 total from all three customers combined per month).
- **Definition:** These are non-associate customers who purchase products for personal consumption. This proves the member is moving products to real end-users, which is the foundation of a stable business.
- **Maintenance:** QA status must be maintained monthly by meeting the minimum purchase requirement from retail customers.

**This "QA" status is the key** that unlocks Pillars 4, 5, 6, and 7.

### 5.0 Pillar 4: Indirect Referral Bonus (Extended Team Commissions)

This bonus extends the Direct Referral concept to your entire downline organization, rewarding you for every package purchase made by associates up to 10 levels deep. Unlike the Harvest Program (Pillar 5), these bonuses are paid **immediately and incrementally** as each package is sold.

**How It Works:** Every time anyone in your 10-level unilevel organization purchases a new associate package, you earn a commission based on their level:

**Key Difference from Harvest:** This bonus is earned per package sold, not based on reaching milestones. It provides steady, incremental income as your team grows.

### 6.0 Pillar 5: The "Package Milestone Ladder" (The Dream)

This new, simplified ladder is based on the "Harvest Program" from the original plan, which rewards **new package purchases** (new associate recruitment). It presents one clear, inspiring path to the plan's "dream" incentives, _unlocked only by Qualified Associates_.

**How It Works:** This is an **all-or-nothing milestone system** - you must reach the exact requirement at each level to earn that level's bonus. The numbers (3, 9, 27, etc.) represent **new associate packages purchased** by your direct recruits in your downline organization. For example:
- You get ₱1,000 + 5-Kilos Rice **only when** you have exactly 3 packages sold by your directs
- You get ₱4,000 + Cellphone **only when** you reach 27 packages sold
- If you have only 26 packages, you receive nothing for Level 3 until the 27th package is purchased

Bonuses are paid upon hitting each milestone, not incrementally as packages are sold.

**Note:** This is separate from the "Residual Income" program (Pillar 5), which rewards ongoing product repurchases.

### 7.0 Pillar 6: Residual Income (The Long-Term Stability)

The V3.0 plan completely revamps the "Unilevel Repeat Purchase P.V. Bonus" to make it simpler and more powerful.

- **1. Retail Profit (New):** "Qualified Associates" earn a 20-30% Retail Profit on all purchases made by their personal retail customers.
- **2. Simplified Unilevel (Improved):** The confusing "Member," "Stockist," and "Wholesale" columns are removed. Instead, all **"Qualified Associates"** earn from one, powerful unilevel table (based on the original "Member" column):
  - **Personal Rebate:** 10% on all personal product purchases.
  - **Level 1-3:** 5%
  - **Level 4-5:** 3%
  - **Level 6-8:** 2%
  - **Level 9-10:** 1%
- **3. Redefined Roles (Improved):** "Stockist" and "Wholesale" are no longer complex commission levels. They are separate, optional business paths for leaders who wish to become regional fulfillment centers, earning a 2-3% fulfillment fee on all orders they service.

### 8.0 Pillar 7: The "Royalty Path" (Sustainable Leadership)

The V3.0 plan redefines leadership. The original ranks are retained, but the high-pressure, high-risk quotas are replaced with "Balanced Growth" requirements.

| Rank           | Original Requirement              | **V3.0 "Balanced" Requirement**                                  | Original Bonus                        |
| :------------- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------ |
| **Supervisor** | 10 Direct Members                 | 10 Direct Members, **5** of whom are "Qualified Associates"      | 3% Group Sales + 5% Repeat Product    |
| **Manager**    | 10 Supervisors + 500k Group Sales | Must be a "QA" with **3** personally-sponsored "Supervisor" legs | 5% Group Sales + 10% Repeat Purchase  |
| **Director**   | 10 Managers + 1M Group Sales      | Must be a "QA" with **3** personally-sponsored "Manager" legs    | 10% Group Sales + 15% Repeat Purchase |
| **Chairman**   | 10 Directors + 5M Group Sales     | Must be a "QA" with **3** personally-sponsored "Director" legs   | 12% Group Sales + 20% Repeat Purchase |

- **Why it Works:** This change makes it _impossible_ to "buy your rank" through inventory loading. A leader can only advance by truly teaching and mentoring their team to acquire customers, creating a healthy and sustainable organization. The progression now culminates in the prestigious "Chairman" rank, clearly distinguishing the highest leadership level.

### 9.0 Pillar 8: The "Active Partnership Bonus" (The Secret Weapon)

This is the strategic "fix" for the plan's most unique bonus. The "Upline Support" bonus—where upline success flows _downward_—is a powerful "sizzle" element. The V3.0 plan keeps this sizzle but removes the risk of passivity.

- **The Bonus (Unchanged):** When an upline has a successful month, their downline shares in that success:
  - Downline Level 1 earns **10%** of Upline's bonus.
  - Downline Level 2 earns **5%**.
  - Downline Level 3 earns **2%**.
- **The "Active Gate" (The Fix):**
  A downline member is **only eligible to _receive_ this bonus** in any given month if they are **"Active & Qualified"** (i.e., they have maintained their 3 personal retail customers).
- **Why it Works:** This transforms a "welfare" bonus into a _powerful activity driver_. Members are highly motivated to maintain their "QA" status (get 3 customers) to ensure they are eligible for the potential big payout from their Upline's work.

### 10.0 Conclusion

This "Balanced Growth" V3.0 plan is a comprehensive model that honors the original plan's strengths while methodically fixing its weaknesses. It retains the fast cash, tangible incentives, and "dream" lifestyle, but anchors them all to the single, sustainable, and legally-sound activity that builds long-term businesses: **selling high-quality products to real customers.** The leadership path now clearly distinguishes each rank, culminating in the prestigious Chairman position, eliminating any confusion from duplicate titles while maintaining the plan's motivational integrity.
