QA21E-03 — A row links to its member, panel and wallet movement
================================================================
Phase 21E — Admin visibility. Runs as admin on the ledger tab.

Steps:
1. Click a credited row's member link on /admin/commissions?ledger=general_harvest.
2. /admin/members/{id} opens with the General Harvest panel showing the same node, position and money.
3. Click the wallet link → the movement row.

Observed:
- Member panel shows position, node, settled state, ten-cell grid and awards table matching the ledger row.
- Wallet link opens the member's statement filtered to that ref_id.

Result: Pass — award → member → ledger row walkable in one click each; panel and movement match the row.
