Notes

- Exactly two tables match `royalty%`.
- The ref_type enum keeps its Phase 17 order and appends `royalty` last; no `royalty_reversal`.
- `royalty_periods` has a natural `PRIMARY KEY (period)` and no AUTO_INCREMENT column.
- `royalty_awards` has the AUTO_INCREMENT id, the `uq_royalty_award (period, user_id)` money anchor,
  the four lookup indexes and the four CHECKs (rank 2–4, non-negative amount, volume pair, rates).
