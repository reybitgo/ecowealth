UX-34 — pinch-zoom on the live layouts
27 November 2026 · headless Chrome 158.0.8127.37 (DevTools protocol)
390 × 844 mobile emulation, deviceScaleFactor 3

Method
  Open the page, read window.visualViewport.scale, dispatch a real two-finger
  touch pinch through Input.dispatchTouchEvent (touchStart with two points,
  eight touchMove pairs spreading 16 px per step, touchEnd), then read the
  scale again. The control page carries the old app viewport
  "width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0, user-scalable=no,
  minimum-scale=1.0, maximum-scale=1.0" to prove the gesture is sensitive to
  the removed cap.

Reading
  The auth page (/login) and the app layout (404, served through
  app_header.php) both zoom under a real pinch; the same pinch on the old
  viewport does not move the scale, so the result comes from the change and
  not from the harness. Screenshots of the same two pages at 390 px:
  ux-34-login-390.png and ux-34-dashboard-390.png.
